# Content rating questionnaire preparation

Use the final app build and policy when completing the IARC questionnaire.

## Expected declarations

- Users can interact with each other: **Yes** — direct conversations, comments, and community posts.
- User-generated content: **Yes** — text, photos, and short videos.
- Content moderation, report, and block mechanisms: **Yes**.
- Digital goods, gambling, real-money games: **No**.
- Location sharing: **No**.
- Sexual content, nudity, hate, threats, profanity, or illegal content: **Not permitted by policy**; report and moderation tools are provided.
- Web browsing/access to unrestricted web content: **No**.

Do not guess the final age rating. Submit truthful answers in Play Console and use the rating it assigns.
