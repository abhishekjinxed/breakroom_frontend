## Full description

Breakroom is a professional community space for employees who want a healthier, more human workday.

Take a short break from the inbox, connect with a verified community member, and share a Work Pulse about what you are working on. Keep up with quick Break Briefs: short, workday video moments designed for a quick reset.

### Features

- Google-verified sign-in
- Professional one-to-one break conversations
- Work Pulse updates with photos, video, applause, and notes
- Break Briefs: short workplace video moments
- Reporting and blocking tools for a safer community
- Terms acceptance, privacy controls, and in-app account deletion

Breakroom is designed for constructive, respectful workplace interaction. Members must follow the Community Standards and can report inappropriate content or block a member at any time.
